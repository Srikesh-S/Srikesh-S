# Employee Data Analysis – Week 1 Day 5

## Problem
Explore employee distribution, salary patterns, age/salary relationship, and joining trends.

## Data
10 synthetic employee records with department, age, monthly salary, and joining date.

## Key insights
1. IT has the largest headcount (4 employees); HR, Finance, and Marketing have 2 each.
2. Monthly salary values range from ₹25,000 to ₹45,000 in this sample.
3. One employee joined in each month from January to September 2025 in this sample.

## Recommendations
1. Review staffing needs department-wise before reallocating employees.
2. Compare compensation using role, experience, and performance in addition to department.

## Limitations
The sample contains only 10 synthetic records and is not representative of a real company. It does not include job level, experience, performance, or attrition.
