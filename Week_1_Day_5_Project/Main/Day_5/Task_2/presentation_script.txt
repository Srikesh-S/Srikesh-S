5-minute presentation script

Good morning everyone. Today I am presenting my Employee Data Analysis project using Python.
The objective is to explore department headcount, salary patterns, age and salary relationships, and employee joining trends. I used a sample dataset with 10 synthetic employee records. Pandas helped with data handling, and Matplotlib and Seaborn were used for charts.
First, the bar chart shows department-wise employee count. IT has four employees, while HR, Finance and Marketing have two each.
Second, the histogram shows monthly salaries ranging from ₹25,000 to ₹45,000 in this sample.
Third, the scatter plot compares age and salary. It suggests a general upward pattern, but age alone does not determine salary.
I also created a joining trend line chart and a salary-by-department box plot.
Based on these sample observations, I recommend reviewing staffing needs by department and comparing salaries with role, experience and performance.
A limitation is that this dataset has only ten synthetic records, so it cannot represent a real company's workforce.
This project helped me understand how charts can turn raw data into readable insights. Thank you.
